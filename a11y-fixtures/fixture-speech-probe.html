NVDA speech-capture fleet probe

This page exists for nvda-fleet-survey.mjs. The survey writes a machine-stamped string into the live region below, then reads NVDA's own log back and checks the parser recovered it — proving the speech-capture layer works on this machine's NVDA rather than inferring it from a version number.

What a red result means

A failed probe means the parser did not recover the string from this machine's NVDA log — most likely a different log format on an older build. That is a successful survey: it is the exact failure the layer exists to catch, and a parser that silently returns nothing is indistinguishable from a page that said nothing.
